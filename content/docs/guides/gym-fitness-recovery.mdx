# Gyms & Sports Recovery Guide

Partner gyms, sports clubs, and crossfit boxes use Rehabify to offer professional injury prevention and recovery services to their members.

---

## How Gyms Earn with Rehabify

1. **Reception QR Codes**: Standee banners at gym reception desks allowing members with sprains, joint pain, or delayed-onset muscle soreness (DOMS) to book instant physio sessions.
2. **Embedded Booking in Member App**: Place our drop-in JavaScript widget inside your gym's mobile or web portal.
3. **Revenue Share**: Earn a percentage commission on every session booked by gym members.

---

## Sample Gym Reception QR Code

Place a banner at the front desk:

> **Injured during a workout?**
> Scan this QR code to get an MRTBN-verified sports physiotherapist at your home or at the gym within 2 hours.
> *Powered by Rehabify Health.*
