# Corporate Wellness & HR Guide

Prolonged desk work, repetitive strain, and back pain account for over 40% of workplace productivity losses. Rehabify empowers HR and People teams to provide direct physiotherapy benefits to employees.

---

## Corporate Setup Options

### 1. Subsidized Care Packages
Companies purchase bulk session credits (e.g. 50 sessions/quarter) allocated across staff members. Employees schedule home or office visits via the corporate WhatsApp link.

### 2. Onsite Ergonomic Audits
Rehabify physiotherapists visit your corporate offices to assess employee workstations, provide posture workshops, and deliver on-demand 30-minute musculoskeletal relief sessions.

### 3. HR Analytics Dashboard
HR administrators receive quarterly de-identified health metrics:
* Most common workplace physical complaints (e.g., *72% Lower Back Pain, 18% Carpal Tunnel*).
* Employee participation and satisfaction rates (CSAT / NPS).
* Average pain reduction score across treated staff members.
