---
title: Guides
description: End-to-end integration patterns for HMOs, hospitals and clinics, employers, gyms, and partners who want to refer without writing code.
---

Each guide uses the same [Partner API v1 contract](/docs/api-reference) and shows the parts that matter most for that kind of partner.

<Cards>
  <Card title="No-code referral links" href="/docs/guides/no-code-referral-links">
    Share a co-branded link or QR code. Patients refer themselves, and you see every referral and commission.
  </Card>
  <Card title="HMOs and insurers" href="/docs/guides/hmo-integration">
    Refer covered members with HMO direct billing, track utilisation, and pull progress reports.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Hospitals and clinics" href="/docs/guides/hospital-clinic-referral">
    Refer patients from your EHR, a referral link or the Partner Portal, and send progress reports back to the referring doctor.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Corporate wellness" href="/docs/guides/corporate-wellness">
    Offer physiotherapy to staff through a private campaign link or sponsored referrals.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Gyms and fitness recovery" href="/docs/guides/gym-fitness-recovery">
    Reception QR codes, member-app links and trainer referrals, with commission on paid bookings.
  </Card>
</Cards>
