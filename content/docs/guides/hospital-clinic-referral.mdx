# Hospital & Clinic Referral Guide

Hospitals and private clinics without in-house physiotherapy departments can use Rehabify as their **outsourced physical rehabilitation department**.

---

## Benefits for Referring Doctors & Hospitals

* **30-Second Hand-off**: Refer via WhatsApp link, Partner Portal, or API.
* **Automated Discharge Summaries**: Receive comprehensive medical progress reports back upon patient discharge.
* **Doctor Incentive Bonus**: Earn ₦500–₦2,000 partner incentive per completed treatment plan.
* **Zero Overhead**: No physiotherapy equipment or full-time therapist staffing required.

---

## Option 1: 1-Click Partner Portal Form

Doctor or hospital reception logs in at `partners.physioaroundme.com` and fills out the 4-field referral form:
1. Patient Full Name
2. Patient Phone Number
3. Clinical Diagnosis (e.g. *Post-Stroke Left Hemiplegia*)
4. Modality Preference (Home Visit or Clinic Visit)

Rehabify handles everything from that point onwards.

---
