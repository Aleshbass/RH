# SDKs & Drop-in Widgets

If you do not wish to build custom referral forms or booking user interfaces from scratch, Rehabify provides pre-built frontend solutions:

---

## Solutions Available

| Solution | Technology | Best For | Setup Time |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **[JavaScript Embed Widget](/docs/sdks-widgets/embed-widget)** | Pure HTML / `<script>` | Gyms, fitness blogs, WordPress, Webflow sites | 10 minutes |
| **[React Component](/docs/sdks-widgets/react-component)** | React 18 / 19 / Next.js | Modern web applications, EHR web portals | 15 minutes |
| **[WhatsApp Deep-Links](/docs/sdks-widgets/whatsapp-deep-links)** | Parameterized URLs | SMS campaigns, doctor prescription flyers, QR codes | 5 minutes |

---

## Key Benefits

* **Zero Backend Code**: Embed our secure, high-conversion booking modal directly on your frontend.
* **Branded & Themed**: Custom primary colors, typography, and partner logos.
* **Automatic Revenue Tracking**: Every booking completed through your widget is tagged with your Partner ID for revenue attribution.
