# Webhook Retry Policy

Rehabify guarantees **at-least-once delivery** for all webhook events. If your endpoint is down, slow, or responds with an HTTP status other than `2xx`, our dispatcher will automatically retry with exponential backoff.

---

## Retry Schedule

We attempt up to **8 delivery retries** over a 24-hour period:

| Attempt | Delay | Cumulative Elapsed Time |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Initial** | Immediate | 0s |
| **Retry 1** | 15 seconds | 15s |
| **Retry 2** | 1 minute | 1m 15s |
| **Retry 3** | 5 minutes | 6m 15s |
| **Retry 4** | 30 minutes | 36m 15s |
| **Retry 5** | 2 hours | 2h 36m |
| **Retry 6** | 6 hours | 8h 36m |
| **Retry 7** | 12 hours | 20h 36m |
| **Final Retry** | 24 hours | 44h 36m |

---

## Idempotency & Duplicate Handling

Because network delays can cause duplicate delivery attempts, **your webhook handler should be idempotent**.

> [!TIP]
> Always store the `X-Rehabify-Delivery-Id` or event `id` in your database. If you receive a webhook with an ID you have already processed, return `200 OK` immediately without re-executing business logic.

---

## Response Timeout

Your webhook endpoint must respond with an HTTP status within **10,000 ms (10 seconds)**. Requests exceeding 10 seconds are terminated and flagged for retry.
