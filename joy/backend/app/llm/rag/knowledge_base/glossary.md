# Clinical glossary

Terms below ground the model in physio language. Red-flag *words* (chest pain,
saddle numbness, sudden weakness) are listed so Joy can talk about them
plainly. They do **not** decide flags — `safety/` evaluates structured
`ExtractedTriage.flags` independently of this file.

## msk
Musculoskeletal: joints, muscles, tendons, ligaments, back and neck pain,
sprain, strain, osteoarthritis. Common after lifting or sport.

## neuro
Neurological physiotherapy and stroke rehabilitation. Includes stroke,
Parkinson, spinal cord injury, multiple sclerosis, and nerve-related
weakness. Sudden speech change or one-sided weakness are *terms* for
grounding only.

## paediatric
Child and adolescent development: delayed sitting or walking, cerebral
palsy, clubfoot, and school-age sports injuries.

## cardiorespiratory
Breathing and endurance after pneumonia, COPD, or heart deconditioning.
Not a cardiac diagnosis.

## womens_health
Pelvic floor, pregnancy-related back or pelvic pain, postnatal recovery.

## post_surgical
Rehab after an operation: joint replacement, fracture fixation, ligament
repair. Progress within the surgeon's protocol.
