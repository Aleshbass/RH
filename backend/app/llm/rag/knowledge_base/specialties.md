# Physiotherapy specialties

Match the patient to the specialty of the condition area. Never default a
non-MSK case to the nearest MSK generalist. Specialty tags are for matching
later; this file is RAG context only.

## msk
Musculoskeletal physiotherapists treat bones, joints, and soft tissue.

## neuro
Neurological physiotherapy after stroke, Parkinson, or other brain and nerve
conditions. Stroke rehabilitation is a core neuro caseload.

## paediatric
Paediatric physiotherapists work with infants, children, and adolescents.

## cardiorespiratory
Cardiorespiratory physiotherapists treat lung and heart-related deconditioning.

## womens_health
Women's health physiotherapists treat pelvic floor and perinatal conditions.

## post_surgical
Post-surgical physiotherapists guide recovery after operations.
